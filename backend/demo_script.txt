Wouldn't it be amazing if non-verbal people could speak?
That is the dream that Silent Speech brings to life.
Silent Speech gives a voice to those who have lost theirs.
This is live translation mode.
Google Translate exists to bridge the gap between languages, but nothing exists to bridge the gap between disabilities.
Mute people can communicate with sign language, but most people don't understand it.
Now, mute people can communicate with spoken words.
This is enabled by a machine learning pipeline.
First, a visual speech recognition model guesses what the user is saying.
Then, an LLM fixes the lip-reading output based on context.
Finally, text to speech outputs what the user is saying.
All of this runs on a server, and WebSockets send audio and video to and from the client.
The coolest part is that this core pipeline enables much more than just streaming live translations.
Have you considered that non-verbal people have never been able to make phone calls?
At least not until now.
